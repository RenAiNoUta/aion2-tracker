Gear route

Fresh 45 → 3,000 iL
Enter it to highlight your current stage.
Combat Power, in order of value
  1. Replace your lowest item level slot first. Item level is the biggest driver of CP.
  2. Enhance Epic and Unique gear to +10. Weapons, armor and accessories can't fail up to +10. Clash Runes are different: only +1 is safe.
  3. Fill all Arcana slots, then upgrade card grades.
  4. Spend every Daevanion Crystal and open more Daevanion nodes.
  5. Stigma: spend Abyss Points on Stigma Shards until your main Stigmas reach level 20.
  6. Pet Genus: Insight 10 on all five boards gives the Unbound by Genus title (+4.5% PvE damage).
  7. Collect titles and wings. Owned ones add stats permanently.
  8. Amplified Enhancement last. It matters mostly at very high CP.

Global thresholds from community guides (Oct 2026). Conquest tiers open at 700, 1,400 and 2,100; Transcendence stages at 1,600, 1,900, 2,200 and 2,500. Korean guides list higher numbers, so ignore those. Raid Relay ↗ · Odealo ↗ · Corpus ↗ · Hard mode ↗

Conquest bosses

5 dungeons
Before every boss
  • Wait for the whole party before pulling. Anyone outside the arena when the fight starts is locked out.
  • Save stagger for the checks. High damage doesn't mean high stagger. Slot a Stagger Stigma (+50) if checks keep failing.
Krao CaveiL 700Final: Ultimate Berk
Route
  1. Left branch: Neglected Gadioton. Right branch: Enhanced Harcon. Both optional, but each kill improves the final cube.
  2. Kill the Krao Seal Guardian to break the seal on Berk's cave.
  3. Leave the red orbs alone; they chase you into the boss rooms.
  4. Sealed Cave: Ultimate Berk.

Ultimate Berk final

  • Wave of Fury (75% and ~25–33%): he jumps to the center and stones rise. Get behind a stone before the 5 s cast ends. Barriers and Salvation don't save you. In the open means dead.
  • 50% stagger check: 9 s to fill the gauge or the party wipes. Falling stones on orange markers stun for 1 s, so keep moving.
  • Red dust around him means Whirl: the targeted player kites along the outer edge, everyone else spreads away.
  • Slam into two swings: melee get behind him.
  • Enrage: a timer shows on his HP bar for the last 60 s. After that he's twice as fast and hits much harder.
  • Hard: only one stone is real. The others crumble before the wave, so watch which one stays.

Gadioton mid-boss

  • At 35% he pulls everyone in, then stuns. Keep dodge stamina once he's under 50%.

Harcon mid-boss

  • Two crossing laser sets. The safe gaps move between the first and the second set, so reposition.

Urugugu CanyoniL 1,400Final: Divine Auldor
Route
  1. Forest path to Guardian Captain Raur near the entrance.
  2. Back out, then the short path to Judge Urahum.
  3. Kill the Urugugu Wind Mage to start the wind stream, then glide up past the trees.
  4. One player holds aggro while another taps the Kisk, then glide over the water to Auldor. Everyone enters before pulling.

Raur

  • "…Strike me!" (around 70% and 30%): stop attacking and run far away. A delayed shockwave one-shots anyone in range.
  • He can pull up to four players in. Get out of the follow-up circle.

Urahum

  • Slams leave pink pools that stay for the whole fight and stack up. Never stand in them; move after every slam.
  • For the multi-slam, the last hit is the heaviest. Run out of range instead of dodging inside it.

Auldor final

  • He vanishes: a marked player gets the landing. Everyone stacks on that player, or they die.
  • Tornadoes root you, which can stop you from stacking in time. Watch their paths while you move.
  • Avoid orange circles (dives) and the fissure his charge leaves behind.
  • At least two players must stay alive to finish the fight.

DraupniriL 700Final: Transcendent Bakarma
Route
  1. Use the Abyss Entrance Control Device. A tank holds aggro while one player channels it.
  2. Predator Saraswati and Phantasmal Lakshmi. Skippable in Conquest, required in Exploration.
  3. Meditation Room: clear the Bakarma Brigade pack.
  4. Blue portal into Bakarma's Subspace. Wait for the whole party.

Transcendent Bakarma final

  • 75% and 25%: the party gets teleported to the edge. Run to the boss and jump each wave slightly early. At 25%, also avoid the whirlpools.
  • Stagger check in those phases: a fail means a wipe and the boss back at 100%.
  • Between phases the floor turns into a red damage zone.
  • Frontal breath: the tank faces it away from the group.
  • Orange circles under players turn into geysers. Step out and spread.

Lakshmi mid-boss

  • "Agony always comes back around!" means 8 s of damage reflect. Stop DPS.
  • Kill the Handmaiden right away.

Saraswati mid-boss

  • Step out of blue pools. She can stand inside one, so pull her clear first.
  • Face the poison cone away from the party.

Fire TempleiL 2,100Final: Kromede's Desire
Route
  1. Path of Atonement.
  2. Burning Temple: Silver Blade Rotan, then Red Spark Ignus. Black Smoke Murute is on the other branch.
  3. After a mid-boss, glide through the Fire Corridor.
  4. Mirror Altar: Kromede's Desire. The cube opens after her.

Ignus mid-boss

  • Green HP bar on you: in ~3 s you drop a lava pool that stays for the whole fight. Go to the edge before it lands.
  • Floor marks, then he dashes from player to player. Spread so each takes one hit.
  • Later he jumps to the middle for a ring (donut) attack, then a chain of leaps. Spread out.
  • The coolant console on the route switches off the arena's burning hazards.

Murute mid-boss

  • Both arms raised: move. Circles appear under everyone and explode a moment later. Block or time a dodge.
  • Below 50%: stomp waves grow outward twice. Block, perfect-dodge or back away.

Kromede's Desire final

  • She rises into the air (75% and 25%): fire walls sweep the arena. Move through the gaps, wave after wave.
  • 50%: a clone appears next to each player and strikes twice. Spread out, then block or dodge.
  • At low HP her flame attack fires twice and projectiles cross the walls.
  • Hard, late phase: everyone is sent to the entrance. Reach the glowing circle in about 14 s or you die. Don't stop to fight clones.

Ferocious Horn DeniL 2,100Final: Ferocious Horn Nuakum
Route
  1. Watchdog Kwapo.
  2. Shining Mau Totem, the gate to the Altar Door.
  3. Necromancer Duanka.
  4. Howling Throne: Nuakum. The cube opens after him.

Kwapo

  • 75% and 25%: he leaps and scatters orbs. Clear the orbs on one side (leftovers explode) and jump the expanding ring.

Totem

  • Get the whole party inside before starting.
  • Grab grenades from the side platforms to clear the adds and break the barrier, then destroy the totem.

Duanka

  • 75%, 50%, 15%: four tethered adds spawn. One player takes the white orb on the north side and kills them before they're absorbed.
  • 65% and 30%, Soul Caller: run outside the big circle right away, avoiding the electric balls.
  • Black AoE that roots a player: others step in to share the damage.

Nuakum final

  • His hits stack bleed. At 3 stacks you drop a bleed pool, so drop it at the edge.
  • Small circle with inward arrows: everyone stacks on the marked player. Big circle with outward arrows: everyone else stays outside.
  • 75% and 55%: a marked player is rooted and a clone dashes at them. An unmarked player blocks the dash, and not the same player twice.
  • ~30%: the marked player lines up the central orb so his throws hit it.
  • Ground scratch: get behind him.
  • Conquest enrage at about 5 minutes.

Transcendence bosses

2 dungeons
Deus Research BaseiL 1,600Final: Atiel
Route
  1. Guard Troopers Captain Gelcos.
  2. Lab Director Nazmun.
  3. Glide down to the platform: Divine Test Subject Atiel.

Gelcos

  • The opening whips are the best stagger window. A greyed-out gauge means he's immune and a charge is coming.
  • He charges the farthest player and leaves an electric field. Drag it to the edge, away from the group. The second dash hits the same player; then dodge the jump.
  • Pull him into a corner so the whip and charge cover less floor.
  • Nine-line slam: step out of the lines, then straight back in for the second slam.
  • Dark zone: step out. The real radius is smaller than it looks.
  • Vortex: move far away, avoiding the electric fields. Don't stand in front of him.

Nazmun

  • The tank pulls him to the far end or a corner.
  • Rotating lasers: jump them. They come in series, so the first beam isn't the last.
  • White ring on a player: it explodes after ~3 s. That player moves away. Several marks means everyone spreads.
  • Don't pull him onto the stone floor at the glide-off point, or the fight resets.

Atiel final

  • Gather the whole party on the platform before pulling.
  • Tether: two linked players run apart, while everyone else stacks on the third, marked player to share the heavy hit.
  • Grab and smash on one player is a one-shot. Sidestep it.
  • The arena edge falls away and his aura pushes outward. Move toward him and stay off the edge. Blue orbs also shove people off.
  • His chain hits players standing close together, so keep some space.
  • Morphic World at 30%: 10 s to stagger him or the party wipes. Save guard breaks for this.

Shattered ArkanisiL 1,600Final: Talisra of the Void
Route
  1. Siliator of Deceit.
  2. Ruthilis of Pain.
  3. Talisra of the Void.

Siliator of Deceit

  • Don't clump up (bouncing blade).
  • From 75%: two players get red lines. They cross paths in an X so the chakrams collide and shatter. Everyone else stays clear.
  • From 60%: chakrams return along their lines. Don't cross several at once, and keep a dodge ready.
  • Big red circle: carry it away from the party.
  • Melee stay behind him (frontal and donut).

Ruthilis of Pain

  • The opening AoE toward the entrance can kill. Don't come in through the middle.
  • Quadrant cleaves leave lightning puddles that kill on contact. Rotate clockwise to the next safe quadrant, never through old puddles.
  • From 95%: a donut jump on the farthest player. Red arena or big donut: get under the boss.
  • Ring after the lightning: jump it. Don't regroup until your own explosion resolves.
  • From 50%: guillotine lines that bleed and knock down. Spread out.

Talisra of the Void final

  • 75% and 30%: colored droplets appear. Pick up your assigned color (purple or white; the wrong one stuns) and drop the matching dome near the boss.
  • When she flashes a color sequence, stand in the matching domes in that order.
  • 50%, 4 spears: destroy the single white spear in front of her before the cast ends, or a lethal explosion hits.
  • 50%, Diamond Jail: shoot the crystal to free the trapped player.
  • Tracking beam: the target keeps moving, away from others.
  • Don't stand in the cone, and jump the ring waves.
